About the clinic

What is Workwise?

Workwise is an industrial physical therapy clinic dedicated to preventing workplace injuries. We partner with companies to analyze job sites and improve office ergonomics — identifying and resolving the physical risk factors tied to specific job tasks, so our partners see fewer cumulative trauma disorders (CTDs) and a healthier, more productive workforce.

Job site & ergonomic analysis

We partner directly with companies and office workers to evaluate physical workstations, computer setups, task repetitions, and physical work demands.

Resolving physical risk factors

We pinpoint biomechanical stressors — poor lumbar support, forward neck posture, improper keyboard positioning — and resolve them with practical solutions.

Reducing CTDs & injuries

By addressing cumulative trauma disorders proactively, we prevent long-term musculoskeletal damage, keep workers pain-free, and boost team productivity.

Specialized clinical practice

What is industrial physical therapy?

"Industrial physical therapy is the application of physical therapy in the workplace to prevent, treat, and rehabilitate work-related injuries and facilitate safe return to work."

Workwise standard of occupational care

  1. 1

    Prevention

    Evaluating desk setups, posture, lighting, and repetitive keyboard motions to eliminate injury triggers.

  2. 2

    Treatment

    Hands-on physical therapy, myofascial release, joint mobilization, and targeted therapeutic exercise.

  3. 3

    Rehabilitation

    Rebuilding muscular stamina, postural endurance, and joint stability tailored to your daily job duties.

  4. 4

    Safe return to work

    Functional capacity testing, graded workload resumption, and ergonomic adjustments to avoid re-injury.

Clinical breakdown

Common cumulative trauma disorders we treat

Typical workplace root cause

Continuous typing, mouse dragging with wrist extension, and resting wrists on hard desk edges.

Workwise clinical solution

Neutral wrist positioning, ergonomic mouse alignment, tendon gliding exercises, and nerve decompression therapy.

Typical workplace root cause

Prolonged screen viewing with the head jutted forward, low monitor height, and unsupported seated posture.

Workwise clinical solution

Monitor height correction, deep neck flexor strengthening, and postural retraining for a neutral spine.

Typical workplace root cause

Extended sitting without lumbar support, poor chair mechanics, and repeated forward bending.

Workwise clinical solution

Lumbar support fitting, core stabilization exercises, and scheduled movement breaks through the shift.

Typical workplace root cause

Reaching for a mouse positioned too far away, and desk or armrest heights that force the shoulder upward.

Workwise clinical solution

Workstation reach-zone adjustment, rotator cuff strengthening, and joint mobilization to restore range.
