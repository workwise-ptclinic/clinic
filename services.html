Specialized clinical programs

What we offer

Workwise provides specialized PT and workplace wellness services designed around the needs of today's office worker.

Physical therapy & rehabilitation

Restore movement, reduce pain, and rebuild strength.

Comprehensive one-on-one clinical evaluation and evidence-based active physical therapy tailored for desk workers, executives, and industrial staff suffering from repetitive strain, back pain, and joint stiffness.

  • Personalized musculoskeletal recovery plan
  • Targeted joint mobilization & soft-tissue release
  • Functional posture and spine realignment

Best for neck pain, shoulder impingement, lower back spasms, sciatica

Ergonomic assessment

Optimize your workstation and work habits.

In-depth clinical ergonomic evaluation of computer workstations, monitor focal heights, keyboard angles, chair mechanics, and task postures to eliminate physical risk factors.

  • Customized workstation adjustment blueprint
  • Rapid reduction of repetitive strain hazards
  • On-site corporate visits or individual consultations

Best for BPO agents, IT professionals, executives, remote work desks

Workplace wellness

Promote movement, posture, and healthy work habits.

Proactive corporate health initiatives including micro-break movement routines, ergonomic awareness workshops, and stretching protocols engineered for sedentary workforces.

  • Boosts daily workforce energy & stamina
  • Reduces midday mental and postural fatigue
  • Cultivates an active culture of injury prevention

Best for entire corporate teams, call center shifts, tech firms

Work conditioning

Build the strength and endurance needed for work.

Targeted functional conditioning regimens designed to restore physical capacity, core stability, and muscular stamina required to comfortably endure long work shifts.

  • Dynamic postural core stability training
  • Task-specific endurance conditioning
  • Prevents end-of-shift muscular exhaustion

Best for prolonged sitting, shift workers, standing desk users

Injury prevention

Identify workplace risks and prevent musculoskeletal injuries.

Proactive job-hazard analysis, biomechanical screening, and cumulative trauma disorder risk detection before physical symptoms develop into chronic disabilities.

  • Early detection of repetitive strain signals
  • Substantial reduction in employee sick leaves
  • Direct savings on corporate healthcare claims

Best for companies seeking lower turnover and healthcare costs

Return-to-work services

Support a safe and progressive return to work.

Structured functional capacity evaluations, phased workload re-entry, and modified duty guidance ensuring recovering employees return safely with zero re-injury risk.

  • Objective functional capacity benchmarking
  • Graduated work hardening schedules
  • Workplace modification recommendations

Best for post-injury or post-surgery employee reintegration
